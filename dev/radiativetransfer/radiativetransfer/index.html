Radiative transfer

Physical units in volume transfer

Volume emissivities are per photon energy in CGS, j_E in erg s⁻¹ cm⁻³ sr⁻¹ erg⁻¹, and absorptivities are in cm⁻¹. observation_energies are in erg, while coordinates and the geodesic affine parameter use the geometrized length unit selected by unit_mass_in_solar_masses. The initialized past-directed momentum has unit energy in the camera tetrad (or the reference frame used by the image plane).

For q = u ⋅ k, E = E0*q, and L = G*M_unit/c² in cm, the path length measured by the plasma is dl = L*q*ds. The volume state stores optical depth and the accumulated invariant intensity J = I_E/E³. Its derivatives are

\[\frac{d\tau}{ds}=\frac{L}{E_0}E\alpha_E,\qquad \frac{dJ}{ds}=\frac{L}{E_0}\frac{j_E}{E^2}e^{-\tau}.\]

The postprocessor restores the observer's energy cubed. Converting a spectrum per energy to one per frequency requires I_ν = h*I_E and F_ν = h*F_E. Do not add another length conversion to CGS coefficients in a model.

Volume integration has no optical-depth cutoff by default (τmax=Inf). A finite τmax terminates a ray only after every frequency exceeds it. Choose and check this approximation against the source function and the desired accuracy; exp(-τmax) alone is not an absolute error bound.
